📋 Exam Pattern & Marking Scheme

  • Login required: create a free account (or log in) to start any mock — this is how your scores get saved and shown in "My History."
  • Sectional timing: each of the 4 sections gets its own 15-minute timer (60 minutes total) — matching the latest SSC CGL pattern.
  • Questions: 100 (4 sections × 25 questions each).
  • Marking: +2 for every correct answer, −0.5 for every incorrect answer, 0 for unattempted/skipped questions.
  • You can navigate freely within the current section, but once a section's time is up (or you move on early), it locks permanently — no going back.
  • The whole test auto-submits once the final section's timer hits zero.

  6. You may change or clear your answer for any question in the active section before moving on.
  7. Once you submit (or time runs out on the final section), you'll see your total score, a section-wise breakdown, and a full answer review.
